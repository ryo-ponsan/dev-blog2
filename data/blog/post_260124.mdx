## Why a small system
Big goals are fragile when the routine is vague. A small system makes progress predictable:

- A fixed time box (20 minutes)
- A narrow scope (one idea)
- A fixed output (5 bullet points, then 1 paragraph)

## The 20-minute loop
1. Write 5 bullet points that capture the idea.
2. Pick the strongest bullet and expand it into a paragraph.
3. End with one question you want to answer tomorrow.

## Example template

```text
Today I noticed:
- 
- 
- 
- 
- 

Best point:

Next question:
```

## Why this works
The constraint is the feature. You will publish more often, learn faster, and build a reliable archive of ideas.

## Next step
Try it for seven days and keep the questions. Your next post already exists in the list.
